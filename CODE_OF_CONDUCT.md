# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FuzeCLI is a developer tool and open-source project where people should be able to contribute, ask questions, report problems, review code, and disagree constructively without harassment or discrimination.

Project participation is expected to be professional, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and community members are expected to:

- Treat other people with respect and patience.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, designs, decisions, and behavior rather than people.
- Assume good faith while asking for clarification when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions relevant to FuzeCLI and its users.
- Respect different levels of experience and learning.
- Protect private information and never publish credentials, secrets, or personal information without permission.
- Follow the project's security practices when reporting vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or targeted abuse.
- Discriminatory or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project infrastructure or community spaces.
- Maliciously impersonating maintainers or contributors.
- Encouraging or facilitating behavior that violates the project's security or safety boundaries.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable moderation or maintainer requests.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed and encouraged when it remains professional.

A contributor may challenge an implementation, architecture, requirement, test result, or design decision. Do so with evidence, reasoning, and a focus on the project rather than the person.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the FuzeCLI maintainers through GitHub. Do not create a public issue containing private or sensitive information.

Security vulnerabilities must be handled according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when necessary to protect the project and its community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where someone is representing or materially participating in the FuzeCLI project, including repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the circumstances and focused on maintaining a safe, productive project environment.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in FuzeCLI, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful, technically focused community.
